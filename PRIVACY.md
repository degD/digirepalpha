# Privacy Policy for DigiRep

**Effective date:** September 30, 2026

DigiRep is a digital repertoire manager for storing and managing songs and repertoire.

This Privacy Policy explains how DigiRep handles information when you use the application.

## Information We Collect

DigiRep does not collect personal information from users for the operation of the application.

DigiRep does not collect device information.

DigiRep does not collect analytics data.

DigiRep does not use third-party services to collect, process, or share user data.

DigiRep does not require users to create an account.

## Song and Repertoire Data

Songs and repertoire data that you create in DigiRep are stored locally on your device.

The Android application and the web application maintain separate local databases.

Your locally stored song and repertoire data is not transmitted to the developer's servers.

## Import and Export

DigiRep allows you to export your local song database as a `db.songs` file and import a previously exported `db.songs` file.

When you export a file, the file is handled by your device and by any application or service that you choose to use for storing or sharing that file.

DigiRep does not receive or control files after you share them outside the application.

## Data Sharing

DigiRep does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

DigiRep does not transmit your locally stored song or repertoire data to the developer.

Any sharing of exported files is initiated by you through the device's available sharing functionality.

## Data Retention and Deletion

DigiRep does not maintain a remote copy of your song or repertoire data.

Your locally stored data remains on your device until you delete it, remove the application, or otherwise clear the application's stored data.

Exported `db.songs` files are separate files controlled by you. You are responsible for deleting those files from your device or from any other location where you choose to store them.

## Data Security

Because DigiRep stores song and repertoire data locally on your device, the security of that data also depends on the security of your device.

You are responsible for protecting your device and any exported backup files from unauthorized access.

## Children's Privacy

DigiRep does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to DigiRep or its privacy practices.

When this policy is updated, the revised version will be published at the privacy-policy URL associated with DigiRep.
