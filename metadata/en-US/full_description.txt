<p>
  DigiRep is a simple, offline-first songbook and digital repertoire manager
  for musicians. Keep lyrics, chord sheets, and song notes organized on your
  device without an account, subscription, advertisements, or cloud service.
</p>

<p>
  Create your personal repertoire, find songs quickly, and edit them in a
  focused chord-aware text editor. Changes are saved automatically, and your
  collection stays in local storage.
</p>

<h2>Features</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Search songs by title and tags</li>
  <li>Create, edit, and delete songs</li>
  <li>Chord-aware editor for lyrics and chord sheets</li>
  <li>Mark words as chords and display them with distinct styling</li>
  <li>Transpose chords up or down</li>
  <li>Adjust the editor font size for comfortable reading</li>
  <li>Edit song titles and organize songs with tags</li>
  <li>Automatic saving while you work</li>
  <li>Light and dark themes</li>
  <li>Export and import locally</li>
  <li>Offline operation with no account required</li>
</ul>

<p>
  On Android, export a backup through the system share sheet and restore one
  through the document picker. Keep a backup in your preferred storage service
  or transfer it to another installation whenever you need to.
</p>

<p>
  DigiRep is open source and built to keep your repertoire simple, portable,
  and under your control.
</p>
